# Valvoline Oil Change Coupons, a Practical Resource

A small static resource about how Valvoline Instant Oil Change coupons really
work, maintained by the valvoilcoupons.us editorial team.

## What is here

- `index.html` — home hub: the store level coupon system explained
- `coupon-types.html` — store coupons, campaign offers, loyalty rewards, standing programs
- `how-to-redeem.html` — a checklist for using a coupon at the store
- `costs-and-saving.html` — what decides the final bill, flat versus percentage discounts
- `which-oil.html` — conventional, synthetic blend, and full synthetic compared
- `faq.html` — expired coupons, military discounts, and other recurring questions
- `about.html` — who maintains this resource and how its information is checked

## Rules this site follows

- Evergreen explanations only. Current offers and expiry dates live on the main
  guide, because they are set store by store and change weekly.
- Claims come from official sources: store pages, campaign pages, and published
  offer terms. Nothing is copied from coupon aggregator listings.
- This is an independent resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
  sponsored by Valvoline, and brand names belong to their respective owners.
